⚙️ How This RAG Pipeline Works Behind The Scenes
STEP 1
Document Chunking

Files are split into 500-character overlapping chunks to preserve local sentence context.

STEP 2
Vector Embedding

Each chunk is transformed into a 384-dimensional dense vector representing semantic meaning.

STEP 3
ChromaDB Indexing

Vectors are stored in ChromaDB, enabling fast cosine distance similarity queries.

STEP 4
Retrieval & Synthesis

The query is embedded, top matching passages are retrieved, and an answer is formulated with citations.
